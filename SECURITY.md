# Security policy

Galaxy Mind (galaxymind.space) welcomes reports of vulnerabilities in the site, its APIs, its client-side code or the curated vendor data.

## How to report

Please report privately, through one of the contacts in our [security.txt](https://galaxymind.space/.well-known/security.txt):

- a direct message to [@GalaxyMind on X](https://x.com/GalaxyMind), or
- a Nostr DM to our [Galaxy Mind profile](https://njump.me/nprofile1qqspwnkez659yrnzx2fu4kzpngtkak7v3sdpuh6jw83key38xru5wrcny0sdm).

Include the affected URL or file, reproduction steps and, if you can, a minimal proof of concept. Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability, test against other people's data, or run load or denial-of-service tests.

We'll acknowledge your report, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you if you'd like.

## Scope

In scope: galaxymind.space and its `/api` routes, and the code in this repository. Out of scope: third-party services we link to (vendors' shops, marketplaces, payment providers), which should be reported to them.
