About Super Saylor

Super Saylor is a free, browser-based Bitcoin platformer game (an unofficial Michael Saylor parody). You play a pixel Michael-Saylor-style hero across 21 levels of Bitcoin history: leap the genesis block, dodge FUD bears, stack sats, and climb to a Peter Schiff summit showdown, unlocking one of the 21 Rules at the end of each stage. Side-scroller controls, Bitcoin history as the stages.

It is completely free. There is no wallet to connect, nothing to sign up for, and no play-to-earn grind; it's a game, built for fun and a little Bitcoin history. It runs in any browser on desktop or phone, keeps playing offline once loaded, and installs to your home screen like an app. Climb the global all-time and Daily Summit leaderboards and share your run.

How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD. Jump with Up, W, or Space. Duck with Down or S.
  • Powers: Shift or J for the dash / STRK wave; E or F to plant the One Chair. Pause with Esc or P.
  • Mobile: a floating joystick plus jump and action buttons appear on touch screens · drag to run, tap to jump.
  • Goal: reach the flag at the end of each level, gather sats, avoid the bears, and survive the boss fights at levels 7, 14, and 21.

Both. It is one self-contained page with touch controls, so it keeps playing offline and installs to your home screen like an app.
How many levels are there?
21 levels, one for each of the 21 Rules, ending at a summit boss fight.
