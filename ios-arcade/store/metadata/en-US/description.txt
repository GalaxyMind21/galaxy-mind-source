Eight bitcoin games that play offline.
No ads, no account, and no wallet.
Scores are play points, not real bitcoin.

Bitcoin Kart. A 3D racer on twelve tracks, with on-screen touch controls.

Super Saylor. A 21-level platformer through bitcoin history. Unofficial parody.

CHOP. Swipe through cubes drawn from a saved real block. Not the live chain tip.

WEN. Date a 90-day slice of saved price history.

The Bitcoin Gauntlet. A hidden stretch of that history against a simple buy-and-hold bot. Not a brokerage.

Node Runner. One-tap jumps over the shape of the price tape.

Timechain. Stack blocks. The points get smaller as you climb.

The HODL Trail. Ten choices through bitcoin history.

Plays with no network after install. No ads, no account, no tracking, and no wallet.
